About Me

I’m Jeremiah Morris‑Wyley, a Digital Media professional passionate about accessible web design, UX, and public‑sector communication. Since February 2024, I’ve worked as a Technical Support Specialist for the City of Groveland, where I manage the municipal website, mobile app, and software systems. I recently earned my B.A. in Digital Media from the University of Central Florida, with a focus on web design, accessibility, and digital storytelling.

Originally from North Carolina and now rooted in Tavares, Florida, I enjoy building inclusive digital experiences that strengthen community engagement. My career goal is to become a webmaster or website manager for a county or state agency, continuing to bridge technology and public service.
